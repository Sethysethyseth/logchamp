Full rethink, second pass

LogChamp Arena

One continuous arena instead of five pages. Every tab is a place, every stat lives on something in the room, and one sheet holds everything you type. This pass adds the coach, the full analytics views, your block, the rest timer and settings, and shows where each thing you already built goes.

Try it

  1. Swipe the arena left and right to walk between places, or use the tabs.
  2. Start your block day on Home, log a set, and watch the board run the rest timer while the lights dip and come back.
  3. Open Analytics and flip between Muscles, Strength, Exercises and Execution. The scoreboard is the chart.
  4. Ask the coach (top right). The answer goes on the chalkboard in the coach's corner.
  5. Change palette in Profile. Same room, new light.
  6. Drag the grip at the top of the sheet for more room, or tap the arena to put the sheet away.

How to get there without losing anything

  • It is a second skin, not a rewrite. Fields, lists, charts and the coach chat are the components you have now, placed in the sheet. The arena replaces page chrome, not features.
  • Ship it as a switch. "Arena view" in Appearance, off by default. The current layout stays in the app, untouched, until you decide otherwise.
  • One place at a time. Home and the logger first, then Analytics, then the rest. Each step is usable on its own.
  • Everything from this week carries over: the crown intro, the pixel tick on a logged set, the lights following a live workout.

Not solved yet

  • Light mode. Probably the same arena by day, with skylights. Not drawn.
  • Long pages. Analytics tables and the coach chat want the sheet at full height, which leaves a thin strip of arena.
  • Desktop. The sheet likely becomes a side panel with the arena beside it.
  • The keyboard. While typing, the sheet has to own the screen. The arena waits.
