LogChamp Arena

The arena look from the last pass, now carrying the three things you asked about: a serious advanced analytics view, blocks from library to logged day, and the whole logger with every setup option.

Walk through it

  1. Logging. On Home, start the block day or an empty workout. Tap a tick to log a set. Try RIR / RPE, Exercise notes, Repeat last, and Edit for the full setup. Switch to Table view. Nudge or skip the rest timer. Discard and Finish both ask first.
  2. Advanced analytics. Open Analytics, then "Advanced view". Change the range, flip the four views, and switch chart to table.
  3. Blocks. Library, Blocks, open "Imported block". Pick a week, start a day, finish it, and watch the program board on the wall and the Home card move on.
  4. Everything else from the last pass still works: swipe to walk, the coach, palettes in Profile.

Advanced analytics

The scoreboard stays the headline. "Advanced view" pulls the sheet up and shows the analytics you already built, at full detail: stimulating and effective sets against the target range, top-set trend with records marked, per-exercise table, execution against plan. Clean lines and real axes, no pixel art in the charts. The arena shrinks to a strip so the numbers get the screen.

In the build these are your existing chart components placed in the sheet, with the 2 / 4 / 8 / 12 week range, chart or table, and "how it's calculated" all kept.

Blocks

  • Home offers the next block day first, as it does today.
  • The block page lives at the rack: weeks across the top, each day with its status, start, pause and edit.
  • The program board on the rack wall is the same block: one cell per day, filled as you finish them.
  • A block day's logger shows the plan on each lift with an "As planned" fill, and counts sets against the plan on the scoreboard.
