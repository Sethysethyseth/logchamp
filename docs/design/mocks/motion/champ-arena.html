Default theme idea for LogChamp

Champ Arena

The city says "night" but not "champ". This one puts you where a champ is made: an empty arena after hours. One lifting platform, a loaded bar, two light towers, the crown banner hanging over it, and stands that fill in as you train.

Same pixel-and-dither language as the skyline, same indigo, so cards, charts and the crown intro all sit on it unchanged.

It reacts to the workout

  • Start a workout: the towers switch on one at a time and the beams find the platform.
  • Finish: camera flashes ripple through the stands for two seconds, in step with the crown stamp on Home.
  • Idle: lights low, dust drifting through the beams. Nothing else moves.

Room to grow

  • Stands fill with your history: one lit seat per workout logged, so the crowd is literally your consistency. Full house at 1,000.
  • The bar is yours: plates on the bar match your last top set.
  • Banners for milestones: 100 / 1,000 workouts hang a new banner in the rafters.

Preview only. Nothing here is in the app yet. It is drawn in code on one small canvas, so it would ship as a still for the scene band plus the MX10 scene-life layer for the motion.
