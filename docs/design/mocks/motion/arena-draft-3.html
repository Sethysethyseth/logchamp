Draft 3 · round 1

LogChamp Arena

Draft 2 with the first phone smoke answered. Every state the build has to reach is drawn here and has a switch above the phone, so nothing is left to a description. The build follows this page and ARENA-ROUND-1.md beside it.

Step through it

  1. Place walks the camera. State sets idle, a live workout, a rest, or the moment after Finish, and holds it still.
  2. View is the stage size: open, the strip, or the full view. The logger and the coach are working pages and keep the strip.
  3. Keyboard raises a stand-in keyboard. Finish sheet and Start picker raise the sheets that slide up. Short phone cuts the frame down to draft 2's height.

Everything in the phone still works by hand: start a workout, log a set, finish, ask the coach, tap the arena.

The stage folds, in one motion

  • Open when you arrive. The arena has the top of the screen.
  • Scrolling folds it. The first stretch of a scroll lifts the page instead of moving its contents, 1:1 with your thumb. Scroll back to the top and it returns the same way.
  • Folded, the strip is the scoreboard. The same board that hangs over the platform, cut close. It fades in place; nothing travels.
  • Back for the moments. Starting holds the room for a second so you see the lights come on. Finishing drops the sheet so you see the flashes.

One motion at a time. The room is drawn once and rides on the sheet's top edge as a single piece, with no gap. The camera's height never changes: it is fixed to the floor. A taller phone does not move the camera or change the pixel size, it shows more roof.

The full view

Tap the arena and the page steps aside. It stays that way from tab to tab, with one line saying what the place knows, until you tap the arena again. The roof fills what used to be empty air, and the balcony says where you are.

Hold a finger on the arena, or pinch, to look closer. Drag to look around, tap to step back. One thing in the crowd behind the platform answers a tap.
