Draft 2

LogChamp Arena

The final draft, checked against the app as it runs today. The stage now steps back while you work and returns for the moments, the week is back on Home, and the full view stays until you leave it.

The middle ground: the stage folds

  • Open when you arrive. The arena has the top of the screen.
  • Scrolling folds it. The first stretch of a scroll lifts the page instead of moving its contents, so the page follows your thumb exactly and the arena slides away under it. Scroll back to the top and it returns the same way.
  • Folded, the strip is the scoreboard. Clock, set and rest while you log, the chalkboard at the coach, the headline of the place everywhere else. Logging, the coach and the advanced view start folded.
  • Back for the moments. Starting a workout holds the room for a second so you see the lights come on. Finishing drops the sheet so you see the flashes.

One motion at a time. The room is drawn once and rides on the sheet's edge as a single piece. It no longer pans on its own while the sheet moves, which is what made full view to half hard on the eyes. The scoreboard strip fades in place. Walking between places now runs at full frame rate.

The full view

Tap the arena and the page steps aside. It now stays that way from tab to tab, with one line saying what the place knows, until you tap the arena again.

Hold a finger on the arena, or pinch, to look closer. Drag to look around, tap to step back. Three things can only be seen up close. One of them is in the crowd behind the platform, and it answers a tap.

The week on Home

"Last 7 days" is back in the Home sheet with everything the current card has: the day strip, workouts, sets, top set, top gain, records, movers and the note. The scoreboard cycles the same numbers, so the week is still on screen when the sheet is folded or put away.

Try it

  1. Scroll Home slowly, then back. The page follows your thumb and the strip keeps the week.
  2. Hold Start empty workout, then let go and tap it. Log a set, change tabs, drag the bar in any direction.
  3. Finish. Then tap the arena and walk the tabs in the full view.
  4. Hold on the crowd behind the platform.
