What to try here

Scroll this page with your thumb starting on the bar. With "Any way" the bar comes with your thumb and the page stays put. With "Sideways" the page scrolls like it does today. That is the one real cost of going every-direction.

What never changes

A tap on the bar still resumes. The X still asks before it discards. Swiping only hides the bar until the app is next opened, and the workout is always on Home.
